## Overview

Polyetherimide (PEI) foams are widely used in aerospace and transportation industries due to their excellent thermal stability and lightweight nature.

This invention provides an improved PEI foam and an optimized preparation methodology that enhances foam quality and performance.

## Key Innovations

* Improved PEI foam architecture
* Enhanced structural stability
* Optimized processing route
* Lightweight engineering material
* Improved manufacturing consistency

## Technical Significance

The developed approach enables production of high-performance polymer foams suitable for demanding engineering environments while maintaining low density.

The invention contributes to improved manufacturing reliability and material performance.

## Potential Applications

* Aerospace interiors
* Lightweight structural panels
* Transportation components
* Energy-efficient structures
* High-temperature engineering systems
