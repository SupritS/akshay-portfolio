## Overview

This invention introduces a flexible and self-healing bio-based vitrimer epoxy foam designed to overcome the limitations of conventional thermoset foams, which are difficult to repair and recycle after damage.

The developed material combines lightweight cellular architecture with dynamic covalent vitrimer chemistry, enabling repairability while maintaining structural integrity.

## Key Innovations

* Bio-based epoxy vitrimer network
* Intrinsic self-healing capability
* Lightweight foam architecture
* Recyclable thermoset system
* Enhanced sustainability

## Technical Significance

The vitrimer network enables bond exchange reactions that allow the material to recover from damage and extend service life.

This approach addresses major challenges associated with conventional thermoset foams used in engineering structures.

## Potential Applications

* Aerospace sandwich structures
* Lightweight transportation components
* Thermal insulation systems
* Structural foam cores
* Sustainable engineering materials
