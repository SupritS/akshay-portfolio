# Repairable & Recyclable Vitrimer Composites

High-performance carbon fiber composites are widely used in aerospace, automotive, and energy sectors due to their outstanding mechanical properties. However, conventional thermoset composites present a major challenge: once cured, they cannot be easily repaired, reshaped, or recycled.

My research focuses on developing vitrimer-based composite systems capable of retaining the mechanical advantages of thermosets while introducing repairability, recyclability, and sustainability through dynamic polymer networks.

## Research Motivation

The growing demand for sustainable engineering materials has intensified the need for composite systems that can be repaired and recycled at the end of their service life.

Traditional thermoset composites often contribute to:

* High maintenance costs
* Difficult repair procedures
* Limited recyclability
* Increased environmental burden
* Material waste generation

Vitrimer chemistry provides a promising pathway to overcome these limitations.

## Research Objectives

* Develop repairable composite systems
* Improve recyclability of carbon fiber composites
* Maintain structural performance
* Introduce dynamic network adaptability
* Enhance sustainability
* Enable circular-material design

## Triple Dynamic Network Strategy

This work introduces a triple dynamic network architecture within vitrimer-based composite systems.

The dynamic network enables:

* Damage repair
* Network rearrangement
* Reprocessing capability
* Shape adaptability
* Improved service life

Unlike conventional thermosets, these materials retain structural integrity while allowing controlled bond exchange reactions.

## Key Findings

The developed vitrimer composite system demonstrated:

* Effective repairability
* Recyclability and reprocessability
* Strong mechanical performance
* Enhanced structural durability
* Improved sustainability profile

The results illustrate the potential of dynamic polymer networks in enabling next-generation composite materials suitable for demanding engineering environments.
