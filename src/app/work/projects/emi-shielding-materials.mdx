# EMI Shielding & Multifunctional Materials

Modern aerospace, defense, and electronic systems require materials that can simultaneously provide structural integrity and protection against electromagnetic interference (EMI). Traditional structural composites offer excellent mechanical performance but often lack additional functionalities required in increasingly connected and electronic-rich environments.

My research focuses on integrating nanostructured materials and engineered interfaces into polymer composites to create multifunctional systems capable of delivering both structural and functional performance.

## Research Motivation

With the rapid growth of wireless communication systems, electronic devices, radar technologies, and aerospace electronics, electromagnetic pollution has become a significant engineering challenge.

Composite materials capable of shielding electromagnetic radiation while maintaining lightweight structural performance are highly desirable for next-generation aerospace and defense applications.

## Research Objectives

* Enhance electromagnetic shielding effectiveness
* Develop multifunctional composite structures
* Improve mechanical performance
* Introduce self-healing capabilities
* Engineer hierarchical nanostructures
* Enable lightweight shielding solutions

## ZnO-Decorated Carbon Architectures

One of the major directions of this work involves the development of ZnO-decorated carbon structures through microwave-assisted synthesis techniques.

These hierarchical structures provide:

* Enhanced electromagnetic wave attenuation
* Improved dielectric behavior
* Increased interfacial interactions
* Multifunctional composite performance

The combination of carbon-based conductive pathways and engineered ZnO nanostructures enables efficient absorption and dissipation of electromagnetic radiation.
